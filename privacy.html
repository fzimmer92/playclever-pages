Privacy Policy for Play Clever

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Play Clever does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share any personal data. All information you enter — player names, match results, and statistics — is stored only in a local database on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Specifically

  • Play Clever has no user accounts and no login.
  • Play Clever makes no network requests of any kind.
  • Play Clever includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • All match data can be permanently deleted by deleting the app from your device.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact support@playclever.eu.
